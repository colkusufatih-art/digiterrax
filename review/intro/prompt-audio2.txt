A cinematic sci-fi spacecraft flyby and landing sound effect. It begins immediately at full loud volume: a powerful spacecraft rushes past very close and fast, a deep whooshing engine roar with a strong sense of directional motion sweeping through, like a doppler effect as the ship passes by. The engine roar continues as the ship decelerates and begins its final descent, the pitch dropping lower and the intensity easing off. Around two thirds of the way through, the ship touches down with a solid, weighty landing thud and a burst of settling thruster hiss. After landing, the engines power down into a low, steady hum that fades out smoothly and completely by the end. Loud, powerful and full of motion at the start, calm and settled at the end. No music, no melody, no vocals, pure cinematic sound design.
