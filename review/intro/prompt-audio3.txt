A short cinematic ambient film score piece for a science-fiction space journey scene. Natural, organic instrumentation: a warm sustained pipe organ or string pad, a steady pulsing rhythmic arpeggio that creates a strong sense of forward momentum and the passing of time, like a heartbeat. The piece builds gradually and steadily in intensity and layers, full of tension and anticipation, arriving at a warm resolving emotional swell about two thirds of the way through. After the swell, the music sustains briefly with a sense of arrival and calm, then fades out gently and completely by the end. Emotional, spacious, epic but intimate, natural orchestral and ambient textures, minimalist and pulsing. No electronic drums, no glitchy digital sounds, no vocals.
