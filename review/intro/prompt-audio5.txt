A cinematic instrumental cue built around a steady, insistent, hypnotic pulsing rhythm on a low organ and piano, like a ticking clock or a heartbeat, present from the very first moment and continuing steadily all the way through without stopping. This pulse is the main character of the piece and creates a constant physical sense of forward movement, momentum, and the passing of time. Sustained strings gradually layer on top of the pulse, rising slowly in register and intensity, adding weight, awe and a slightly melancholic hope, without ever collapsing into one single big release. The mood is vast, minor-key, patient and relentless, epic and cinematic, like the opening of a long space journey. Toward the end the strings grow warmer and fuller while the pulse continues underneath. Natural orchestral instruments only: pipe organ, piano, low strings, cello, double bass. No drums, no electronic beats, no synth pads, no vocals.
